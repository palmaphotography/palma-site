A little about me

Hi, I'm Alina.

I'm the photographer behind PALMA. Photography has been part of my life for almost ten years. What began as a hobby grew into something I couldn't imagine my life without, eventually leading me to study at the New York Film Academy.

I'm drawn to everything that happens between the perfectly posed moments: a glance, a movement, the way light falls on someone's face, or an unexpected laugh.

Sometimes the photographs are bold and editorial. Sometimes they're quiet, intimate, and beautifully human. I love both.

PALMA began in sunny Florida. Today, I'm based in Seattle, creating space for new faces, new stories, and photographs that feel like you.

With love, Alina
